MusicBrain builds open-source MIDI brains for musicians who want one box to
rule their rig — without vendor lock-in.

## Open source, on purpose

The firmware, the editor and the simulator are open. You can see how your
gear works, fix what bugs you, and keep using it long after we would ever
stop selling it.

## Not MusicBrainz

We get it a lot: [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) is the excellent open
music encyclopedia. We are MusicBrain — hardware for your pedalboard. Fans of
their work, no relation.
